What we do

Bolding & Bruggeman ApS is a Danish company specialising in the development and application of numerical models for aquatic systems. We combine decades of experience in physical oceanography, marine biogeochemistry, and scientific software engineering.

Our work spans from fundamental model development to operational forecasting systems, always with an emphasis on open, reproducible, and well-documented software.

Expertise

  • Biogeochemical model development (FABM)
  • Turbulence & mixing parameterisation (GOTM)
  • Coupled physical–biogeochemical simulations
  • Lake and coastal water quality modelling
  • Model calibration & uncertainty quantification
  • Open-source scientific software architecture
  • Copernicus Marine Service integration
